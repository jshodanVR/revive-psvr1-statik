WINDOWS PCVR / OPENXR TARGET
7 October 2026

The target is Windows PCVR with an OpenXR runtime, independently of headset
brand. Pimax Dream Air is the user's test headset, not a project requirement.
The upstream author's Virtual Desktop/VDXR setup is another tested setup,
not an architectural dependency. No Android/standalone Quest build is involved.
The renderer currently requires XR_KHR_vulkan_enable. Runtime/device discovery
alone does not establish correct stereo, input, audio or frame pacing.

HEADSET AND CONTROLLER COMPATIBILITY ARE SEPARATE
Head/eye poses, field of view and presentation come from OpenXR. Tracked VR
controllers use OpenXR action profiles, not Pimax-specific code and not optical
hand tracking. A physical DualShock/DualSense uses the separate PC gamepad path;
without a tracked pose, its virtual position uses the existing fallback.

New source bindings: Oculus Touch, Valve Index, Microsoft motion controller,
HTC Vive controller, and native Valve Frame (when its extension is offered). A rejected profile no longer prevents trying other ones.
The runtime can expose a compatible profile for other controller models. Logs
report the active profile for each hand; the advertised controller profile,
rather than the headset's brand, determines which mapping applies.
Legacy mappings pass bundled Khronos registry path/type checks; Frame uses an
independent fixture from Valve's published paths because this SDK predates it.
They have NOT all been exercised on physical devices. Unknown profiles and
controllers with fewer controls can need additional bindings or compromises.
Do not advertise universal compatibility as already verified.

NATIVE FRAME MAPPING
The full-gamepad mapping applies to the shared emulated DualShock path across
titles, not only Statik. Preserve separate physical D-pad and face buttons,
both analog sticks and clicks, bumpers, triggers, Menu and View. Native Frame
must not inherit reduced-controller shortcuts that replace the right analog
stick with a D-pad. Touchpad gestures remain an emulation feature, not a
physical DualShock touch surface on Frame.
The runtime must offer XR_VALVE_frame_controller_interaction and activate
/interaction_profiles/valve/frame_controller_valve. If it uses Touch fallback,
Frame's D-pad and extra face buttons cannot all be distinguished.
- Right A/B/X/Y = PS4 Cross/Circle/Square/Triangle; left D-pad = PS4 D-pad.
- Bumpers = L1/R1, triggers = L2/R2. Grips remain available for Move/Aim.
- Both sticks = PS4 analog sticks, clicks = L3/R3. Both clicks recenter.
- Right Menu = Options. Left View = touchpad click; hold View and move the
  right stick to drag a finger on the touchpad. Without View it stays analog.
- In two-Move mode, left D-pad and right ABXY are the four face buttons for
  each independent Move; triggers/grips remain Trigger/Move.
- The WipEout/Thumper legacy stick-to-D-pad override does not replace Frame's
  native right stick. The physical D-pad remains independent.
Source: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/steamframe/input

MIXED PC GAMEPAD AND VR CONTROLLERS
Choose Automatic mode. Both sources can stay connected. Button ownership
follows deliberate input with echo suppression for streaming-generated pads.
VR position tracking continues when the PC gamepad supplies buttons. It does
not require optical hand tracking. Focus/tracking loss still releases poses.
For the non-Astro DualShock bridge, press a physical pad button and deliberately
turn that pad to restore its gyro and the configured virtual position. Mere
resting gyro noise does not take over. Buttons alone retain mixed VR tracking.
Use a face/menu button, stick or deliberate trigger pull to switch back to VR
buttons and tracked motion. PC gamepad only mode intentionally disables VR
controller input. This motion-ownership policy still needs physical acceptance.

LEGACY GAMEPAD / AIM MAPPING
- Touch: right A=Cross, B=Square; left X=Circle, Y=Triangle.
- Index: right A=Cross, B=Square; left A=Circle, B=Triangle.
  Press left trackpad for Options; system buttons remain owned by the runtime.
- Microsoft motion: sticks remain sticks; click right trackpad center/down
  for Cross, left for Square, right for Circle, up for Triangle.
- Vive: pads replace sticks; right pad clicks use the same face-button sectors.
  A pad click can therefore also move its axis. Vive lacks separate stick clicks,
  so L3/R3 and the dual-stick recenter shortcut have no equivalent in this map.
- Left/right trigger=L2/R2; grips=L1/R1; left menu=Options where available.
- For titles using two Move devices, each controller's pad sectors produce the
  face buttons of that Move. Trigger/grip remain Trigger/Move independently.
- In ordinary gamepad emulation the right stick drives the virtual touchpad,
  except the updated Statik layout below and titles with a directional-pad override. In Aim mode it
  supplies the real right analog stick and R3, not a virtual touchpad.

FARPOINT AIM (EXPERIMENTAL)
Disconnect PC gamepads, select 'Two VR controllers: Aim gun (experimental)'.
The right controller is the rear hand by default. Hold the left grip and place
the left hand ahead of it to steer one weapon from both tracked hands. Releasing
the support grip or losing support tracking returns to one-handed pointing.
Loss of primary tracking does not fabricate a still-tracked gun.
The real OpenXR path uses grip positions for both hands, with the primary aim
orientation for one-handed pointing, avoiding mixed profile-dependent origins.
Farpoint sees one Gun/Aim device, not two Move devices. Menus in the room require
aiming at the button and pulling the right trigger; repeated Cross is insufficient.
The fixed virtual-gamepad distance controls do not position the tracked Aim.
Physical alignment, haptics, movement, reload and sustained play remain to test.

STATIK COMPLETE GAMEPAD LAYER - 9 OCTOBER 2026 r2
Applies to the r2 Statik player only; Frame keeps its native mapping above.
Touch/Pimax: right A=Cross, B=Square; left X=Circle, Y=Triangle.
Index: left A=Circle, B=Triangle; left trackpad press is Menu/modifier.
Triggers L2/R2; grips L1/R1. Both sticks analog; clicks L3/R3.
Hold Menu: left stick becomes D-pad, right stick controls touchpad finger,
right stick click presses touchpad. Release after using this layer does not
pause. A plain Menu tap sends Options on release.
Statik/WipEout fallback Menu: hold left stick click and press right A.
The chord consumes Cross/L3. In Statik it can also be held as the modifier.
Both stick clicks outside the modifier recenter. PC pad: hold Options/Start
for one second to recenter view and virtual-pad yaw. PS/Share are not gameplay
buttons. Vive wands lack independent stick clicks and cannot cover every input.
Physical Pimax/Index first-puzzle coverage remains unverified by these tests.

TRACKED HAND SELECTION
Each applicable launcher offers right/left for virtual gamepad or Aim pose.
Default is right. Buttons stay mapped to their physical hands. In Aim mode,
use the opposite grip for support. This setting has no effect in PC gamepad
only mode or on an independent emulated Move pair.
