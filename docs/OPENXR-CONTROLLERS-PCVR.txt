STATIK OPENXR CONTROLLERS

Select your headset's OpenXR runtime before launching Statik. Compatibility
is determined by the runtime and active interaction profile, not headset branding.
A supported binding suggestion does not prove that a physical device was tested.

Use a DualShock 4/DualSense connected to the PC or a pair of tracked VR controllers.
Choose the tracked hand in the launcher. Automatic input permits PC gamepad
buttons and VR tracking to be used together. Steam Frame has dedicated bindings
for its gamepad-style input set. Device-specific behavior still needs testing.

On controllers with fewer buttons, hold Menu to use the left stick as the
D-pad and the right stick for the touchpad. The in-game controls image shows
the full mapping. See CONTROLS.md for recentering and input details.

If input fails, collect logs and report the headset, controllers, OpenXR runtime,
active controller profile, whether a PC gamepad was connected, and whether the
failure affects buttons, sticks, tracking or a combination of these.
