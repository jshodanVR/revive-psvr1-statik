STATIK SUPPORT DIAGNOSTICS

After a problem, close the game and run Collect PCVR Logs.bat. The collector
creates output/PCVR-support-<timestamp>.zip without uploading anything.
Review the archive before sharing: logs may contain local paths and device names.
Game packages, extracted game payloads, saves and shader dumps are excluded.

Launcher diagnostics record the player SHA256, Windows/PowerShell versions,
GPU/driver, OpenXR runtime candidates, effective settings and cached optics.
The emulator log records the runtime actually used, headset/session state,
per-eye resolution and field of view, tracking, controller profiles and errors.
The collector also preserves available previous logs and recent importer metadata.
A missing controller-profile line alone is not proof of a disconnected device.

Describe the symptom, puzzle or transition, headset/controller/runtime,
rendering scale, Enhanced setting and whether a PC gamepad was connected.
Use a short video or consecutive frames when reporting flicker. One screenshot
cannot establish whether an image is stable over time.
